Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game RunFirst (“the App”, package com.sotan.runfirst) handles information.

Who we are

The App is published by Sotan. Contact: gtansu.tansug@gmail.com.

Information the App collects

  • No account or sign-in. The App does not ask for your name, email, or any personal profile.
  • Local game data. Best times and rankings are stored only on your device (Unity PlayerPrefs) and are not uploaded to us.
  • Advertising. The App shows ads through Google AdMob. To serve ads, Google and its partners may collect and process device identifiers (such as the Advertising ID), approximate location derived from IP address, and ad-interaction data. See Google’s policy: policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
  • Consent (EEA/UK). Where required, the App uses Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request your consent for personalized ads before any ad is requested.

How information is used

Device/ad data is used by Google AdMob to display and measure ads. We do not sell your data.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13. AdMob and Google Play “Target audience” settings are configured accordingly, and personalized ads are not enabled for children.

Data retention & deletion

Local data stays on your device and is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its storage. Ad-related data is retained per Google’s policies.

Your choices

  • Reset or limit ad personalization in your device settings (Ads / Privacy).
  • In the EEA/UK you can change consent through the in-app privacy options (Settings › Privacy options).

Changes

We may update this policy; the “Last updated” date will change accordingly.
